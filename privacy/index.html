Twin Skeletons LLC ("we," "us") is a software studio that builds software for small businesses. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. It applies to our website and to our services, including the AI front desk service, which answers calls for small businesses, takes messages, and sends summaries to the business owner. Twin Skeletons LLC is responsible for the information described in this policy.

Information we collect

From callers to businesses that use the AI front desk service:

  • Call details, such as the date, time, length of the call, and the number called
  • Caller name and phone number
  • The message the caller leaves, including the reason for the call and the best time to call back
  • Call recordings or transcripts, where the business has enabled them

From business owners who use our services:

  • Contact information, such as name, business name, email address, and mobile number
  • Account and setup details, such as business hours and CRM connection settings

From website visitors:

  • Information you choose to send us by email
  • Basic technical data, such as browser type and pages visited

How we use information

We use this information to:

  • Answer calls and take messages on behalf of the business
  • Send the business owner call summaries and callback notices by email or text message
  • Pass call details to the business's existing CRM, when the business connects one
  • Provide customer support and respond to questions
  • Keep our services secure, working, and improving
  • Meet legal obligations

Text messaging (SMS)

Text messages are sent by Twin Skeletons LLC. If you give us your mobile number and agree to receive texts, we may send you messages related to our services, such as call summaries and callback notices.

  • Message frequency varies.
  • Message and data rates may apply.
  • Reply STOP to unsubscribe at any time.
  • Reply HELP for help, or email us at jimpowell20@gmail.com.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Mobile numbers, text messaging opt-in data, and consent are not sold, rented, or shared with any third party for their marketing or promotional use. We may share this information only with subcontractors and service providers that help us deliver our services, such as messaging providers and customer support.

How we share information

We do not sell personal information. We share information only:

  • With the business the caller was trying to reach
  • With the CRM or other tools the business chooses to connect
  • With service providers that help us run our services (for example, phone, messaging, hosting, and support providers), who may use it only to provide services to us
  • When required by law, or to protect the rights and safety of our users and others
  • As part of a merger, sale, or similar business change, under the same protections described here

How long we keep information

We keep information only as long as needed to provide our services, support our customers, and meet legal and business requirements. When it is no longer needed, we delete it or make it anonymous. A business that uses the AI front desk service can ask us to delete its call data.

How we protect information

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards to protect information. No system is perfectly secure, so we cannot promise absolute security.

Your choices

  • You can stop text messages at any time by replying STOP.
  • You can ask to see, correct, or delete your information by emailing us.
  • Callers who want a message removed can also contact the business they called.

Children

Our services are meant for businesses and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the effective date above. Continued use of our services means you accept the updated policy.
