Our first product: an AI front desk

Our AI front desk answers your calls when you can't, takes a clear message, and sends you a quick summary. It works with the CRM you already use, so nothing about your setup has to change.

  • Answers your calls. Every caller gets a friendly, professional greeting.
  • Takes complete messages. Caller name, callback number, reason for the call, and the best time to call back.
  • Keeps you in the loop. You get a summary of each call, and the details can flow into your existing CRM.

Text messages

We only send text messages to businesses and callers who give us their mobile number and agree to receive texts, such as call summaries and callback notices. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to unsubscribe or HELP for help.
